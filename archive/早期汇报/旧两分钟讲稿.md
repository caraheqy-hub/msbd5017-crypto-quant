## 第 1 页：问题与项目目标（约 35 秒）

Our project asks a practical question: can a crypto research team trust a reported backtest return? A profitable curve alone does not show whether signals used future information, or whether cash and fees were handled correctly. We use Argo to compare strategies, check the implementation, and prepare records that others can verify. Our users are crypto researchers and investment reviewers.

中文提示：先讲实际使用者为什么不能只看收益曲线，再介绍我们做策略比较、检查实现、保存可验证记录。不要先介绍六个 bug 或 Merkle 树。

## 第 2 页：策略与基准对照（约 45 秒）

We begin with a simple moving-average rule, then compare EMA and Williams signals against holding Bitcoin and Ether. All use the same data, capital and trading fee. MA60 returned 114 percent, versus 14.5 percent for the baseline, with a smaller drawdown. Market exposure also differs: after recent Bitcoin declines, MA60 was invested on eleven of 329 days, while Williams was invested on 303. These are selected historical examples, not evidence of future performance.

中文提示：只口头读 MA60 与基准两组收益，不逐格念表。用 11 天和 303 天说明，同一市场状态下策略持仓很不同。表中的 pp 是百分点，比较的是累计收益差；基准是自建的 BTC/ETH 初始各半持有组合，不是正式指数。

## 第 3 页：平台改进、验证与交接（约 40 秒）

While using Argo, we found implementation details that affected results. We addressed execution timing, cash and fee checks, and indicator and quantity edge cases. A separate Python implementation then reproduced all 730 trades from prices and rules, including quantities, prices and fees. Engineering review of the exact Argo build is still pending. We would welcome advice on additional stress tests for practical use. My teammate will now explain how we make the records externally checkable.

中文提示：强调已完成的独立重放；730 = 168 + 390 + 172。不要说已经证明盈利有效，也不要说新包已经上链。最后一句停顿后交给同学。
