## 第二页：问题与痛点

**中文**

一般做回测，我们先准备行情、写交易规则，再运行策略、分析结果，最后把研究交给别人检查。Argo 已经支持这些基本步骤。实际使用时，我们还需要花时间查看一笔交易为什么发生，解释策略表现为什么不同。同时，我们也发现了现金更新和手续费校验的问题。

**English**

Research usually involves preparing data, defining rules, running backtests, analyzing results and sharing findings. Argo supports these steps. In practice, checking individual trades and explaining performance differences required additional work. We also found issues with cash updates and fee checks.

## 第三页：方案与贡献

**中文**

首先，我们在本地版本修正了这些问题，并增加测试。对于收盘后才能决定买卖的策略，我们还增加了下一天开盘成交的方式。

其次，我们补充了分析工具：把策略和同一套买入持有基准比较，分别看不同市场情况下的表现；再看投了多少资金、多少天没有持仓，以及价格涨跌和手续费分别带来了多少盈亏。

**English**

First, we fixed these issues in our local version and added tests. We also added next-bar open execution for strategies that decide whether to trade after the close.

Second, we built analysis tools to compare strategies with a common buy-and-hold benchmark, examine performance across market conditions, and show exposure, time out of the market, and price gains or losses separately from fees.

## 第四页左侧：进度、下一步与交接

**中文**

目前，我们用 MA60、EMA20 和 Williams 三个案例，检查修改后的计算，并展示新增分析。

我们另外写了一套 Python 程序，先不看 Argo 的交易表，从行情和规则重新算一遍。最后，730笔成交的日期、方向、数量、价格和手续费都能对上。这说明这些案例的计算可以复现，不代表策略未来一定盈利。

接下来，我们会复核所用的 Argo 版本，把工具接入现有流程，增加自动文件检查和更多测试，并实际体验其他平台。时间允许的话，也希望在 Argo 已有记录上，补充因子和策略的研究备注、版本关联。

最后，我们把配置、交易和每日账户净值整理成审计包。接下来由我的同学介绍，怎样确认别人收到的包还是我们交付时的那一份。

**English**

We used MA60, EMA20 and Williams to check the modified calculations and demonstrate the analysis tools. We also wrote a separate Python program that calculated signals and trades from prices and rules, without reading Argo's trade records first. All 730 trades matched in date, direction, quantity, price and fees. This checks calculation consistency, not future profitability.

Next, we will review our Argo build, integrate the tools into its workflow, automate source-file checks, expand testing and try competing platforms. If time permits, we may extend its existing records with research notes and version links for signals and strategies.

Finally, we package the settings, trades and daily account values for review. My teammate will explain how we check that the delivered package has not changed.
