## 封面：标题、团队与用户

中文：我们做一个基于Argo的策略研究工具，帮助加密资产研究者检查、比较和复现回测实验。团队成员和主讲人为HE Qiyuan和ZHANG Xinyue。

English: We are building a research toolkit on Argo to help crypto strategy researchers inspect, compare and reproduce the backtests they deliver.

## Slide 1：问题与痛点

中文：研究者审核回测时，不仅需要收益曲线，还需要检查一笔交易的依据、解释两次实验的变化，并复现交付结果。我们使用Argo时，发现成交时序、指标与账户计算需要进一步核对，现有输出仍需要分别检查和人工关联。我们希望减少这些核对工作，让审核证据更清楚。这是针对当前研究流程的改进，并不是说其他平台没有回测功能。

English: A research reviewer needs to check why a trade happened, understand changes between experiments and reproduce a delivered result. A performance curve alone is not enough. In our use of Argo, execution timing, indicators and account details needed additional checks. The gap is in connecting and checking the evidence for this task, not a claim that existing platforms cannot backtest.

## Slide 2：方案、贡献与价值

中文：方案页用功能表区分基础能力和我们的贡献。Argo提供执行引擎、指标和项目文件。我们在自己的分支修正成交、现金和精度行为，补充按日期查看、同口径基准和市场状态分析，以及基础实验记录和审计包。MA60、EMA20和Williams是不同规则的验收案例，不以高收益作为创新点。上链登记与核验仍待工程集成，由同学补充具体方案。

English: Argo provides the execution foundation. Our contribution is to improve and verify calculation behavior and connect research analysis with reviewable records. The table separates that foundation from our work. We have account corrections, command-line date inspection, common baseline and market-state analysis, and basic record comparison. Three strategies are acceptance examples, not investment recommendations. On-chain version registration remains an integration task for our teammate. General Python strategy input and an interactive interface are still planned.

## Slide 3：进度、挑战与反馈

中文：当前三个案例从行情和规则独立重算，共730笔成交，在日期、方向、数量、价格和费用上全部一致。命令行检查、基础记录和审计包已经可运行。下一步是完善原型、核实Argo构建版本，并接入Hardhat和链上核验。主要挑战是让代码、数据与交付版本保持对应。我们希望请教：成本压力测试、新时段验证和可复现性检查，哪类证据应优先？接下来由同学介绍交付版本的外部核验。

English: Starting from prices and rules, independent calculations matched all 730 trades in date, direction, quantity, price and fees. This is calculation consistency, not evidence of future profitability. Command-line inspection, basic experiment records and audit packages are available. Next are prototype refinement, exact-build engineering review and blockchain integration. A key challenge is keeping the input, code and delivered version aligned. Which evidence should we prioritize: cost stress tests, fresh-period validation, or reproducibility checks? We can reproduce the calculations. My teammate will explain how another reviewer can verify the exact version delivered.
