## 中文版

### 第1页：产品与痛点

我们做一个工具，让策略研究者能复算、检查、追溯自己交付的回测结果。

我们的用户是加密资产策略研究者，以及需要审核研究结果的团队成员。只看一条收益曲线，他们还无法知道某天为什么交易，修改参数后结果为什么变化，也无法确认别人能否复现同一次实验。

因此，我们基于 Argo 回测框架，把策略执行、结果分析和实验记录连接起来。三个策略是用来检验研究功能的案例，而不是投资推荐。

### 第2页：用一个真实交易日说明功能

研究者需要把规则和实际交易对应起来。这里是命令行原型中的一个例子：2022年3月2日，以太坊收盘价低于60日均线，规则因此产生卖出信号。交易发生在第二天开盘，而不是使用已经过去的价格成交。

我们可以分别查看指标值、信号理由、成交数量、价格、手续费和成交后现金。这能帮助研究者检查“为什么交易”以及“是否按规则执行”。同样的流程也支持EMA和Williams案例。

目前已有命令行查看、基础实验记录和比较，交互界面和通用策略接入仍待开发。

### 第3页：一致性证据、反馈与交接

在研究过程中，我们修正了成交时序和账户计算等问题，并从行情和规则出发，用独立Python程序重新计算。三个案例共730笔成交，在日期、方向、数量、价格和费用上全部一致。这是计算一致性的证据，不代表策略未来一定盈利。

每个案例还有一份审计包，保存配置、文件指纹、成交和每日账户净值。下一步我们会完善研究原型，并完成Argo工程复核与链上集成。我们希望请教：在策略审核流程里，成本压力测试、新时段验证、可复现性检查，哪类证据最该优先？

我们已经能复算结果。接下来由我的同学介绍，另一位审核者如何核验我们交付的具体版本。

## English version

### Slide 1 — Product and problem

We are building a tool to help strategy researchers reproduce, inspect and trace the backtests they deliver. Our users are crypto researchers and people reviewing their work. A return curve alone does not explain why a trade happened, what changed between experiments, or whether another person can reproduce the result. Built on Argo, our tool connects execution, analysis and experiment records. Three strategies serve as acceptance examples, rather than investment recommendations.

### Slide 2 — One date, one decision

A researcher needs to connect a trading rule to an actual transaction. Here is a working command-line example. On March second, Ether closed below its sixty-day moving average, so the rule produced a sell signal. The trade was filled at the next opening price, not at a price that had already passed. The output also shows quantity, fee and account cash. The same workflow supports EMA and Williams cases. We have CLI inspection and basic experiment recording; the interactive interface and general strategy input are still planned.

### Slide 3 — Evidence, feedback and handoff

We corrected execution and account issues. Starting from prices and rules, independent calculations matched all 730 trades in date, direction, quantity, price and fees. This demonstrates calculation consistency, not future profitability. Each case also has an audit package containing configuration, file hashes, trades and daily account value. Next are prototype improvements, engineering review and blockchain integration. For strategy review, which evidence should we prioritize: cost stress tests, fresh-period validation, or reproducibility checks? We can reproduce the calculations. The next question is how another reviewer verifies the exact version we delivered.
